# Explanation

Discussion of how the project is put together and why. These pages are for
reading away from the keyboard — they take a wider view than a how-to and are
allowed to weigh alternatives, admit trade-offs, and give opinions.

Explanation is where the *why* lives. If a how-to or reference page starts
justifying itself, the justification belongs here instead.

- [Architecture](architecture.md) — the shape of the codebase and its boundaries

For decisions with a date and a status attached, see the
[decision records](../adr/index.md).
